LOG 03 | THE ARCHIVES

MARS MISSIONS IN A NUTSHELL

Explore how space technology has evolved from early flyby probes to advanced rovers and future human habitats. Every mission requires tough trade-offs—balancing rocket weight, fuel, and scientific gear—to safely carry out each phase of the journey, from launch to landing. Check out the timeline below to see how humanity is paving the path to the Red Planet!

1965

MARINER 4

NASA's Mariner 4 arrives and sends 21 photos of Mars back to Earth.

1971

MARS 3

USSR's Mars 3 lander completes a soft landing, but is lost after several seconds.

1971

MARINER 9

Discovers massive volcanoes and Valles Marineris from orbit.

1976

VIKING 1 & 2

Landers touch down to study the surface environment and search for data.

1997

PATHFINDER & SOJOURNER

Sojourner becomes the first wheeled rover to drive on another planet.

1997

GLOBAL SURVEYOR

Arrives in orbit and maps the Red Planet from pole to pole.

2001

MARS ODYSSEY

Begins a multi-decade mission relaying science data back to Earth.

2003

MARS EXPRESS

ESA orbiter stays active in service for over 19 years.

2004

SPIRIT & OPPORTUNITY

Twin rovers find geological evidence of past flowing water.

2006

RECONNAISSANCE ORBITER

Relays more high-res data than any previous Mars mission.

2012

CURIOSITY ROVER

Lands at Gale Crater to detect organic compounds in the crust.

2014

MARS ORBITER MISSION

India's interplanetary probe successfully reaches Mars orbit.

2016

TRACE GAS ORBITER

Analyzes the precise composition of the Martian atmosphere.

2018

INSIGHT LANDER

Probes the deep interior structure of Mars for the first time.

2021

HOPE & TIANWEN-1

The UAE's Hope orbiter and China's Tianwen-1 arrive at Mars.

2021

PERSEVERANCE & INGENUITY

Perseverance lands alongside the first powered helicopter on another world.

2022

INSIGHT'S FINAL TRANSMISSION

The InSight lander concludes its historic mission after mapping deep marsquakes.

2024

EXOMARS & SAMPLE CACHE

Orbiters and rovers continue mapping atmospheric gases and caching rock samples.

2026
